This website is built and hosted using GitHub Pages, with Claude assisting me throughout the coding and development process. I use AI to help write and refine code, troubleshoot issues, and bring my ideas to life while learning along the way.

My goal isn't to let AI make every decision, but to use it as a tool to work more efficiently. I want the website to reflect some of my own preferences, ideas, and personality, so every edit is guided by my judgment and attention to detail. Whether it's adjusting the layout, refining a feature, or changing a small visual element, I review the results and make sure they align with my vision.

Ultimately, this is a collaborative process between human creativity and AI assistance, with GitHub Pages providing the platform and me maintaining control over the final product.
