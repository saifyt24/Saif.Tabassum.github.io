This website is built and hosted using GitHub Pages, with Claude assisting me throughout the coding and development process. I use AI to help write and refine code, troubleshoot issues, and bring my ideas to life while learning along the way.

My goal isn't to let AI make every decision, but to use it as a tool to work more efficiently. I want the website to reflect my own preferences, ideas, and personality, so every edit is guided by my judgment and attention to detail. Whether it's adjusting the layout, refining a feature, or changing a small visual element, I review the results and make sure they align with my vision.

I'm aware that the website could be considerably more advanced, polished, and feature-rich if I chose to invest more time and resources into it. However, I've intentionally kept the scope appropriate for its purpose: a personal portfolio to showcase my projects, experience, and interests. Using GitHub Pages allows me to host it for free without paying for a custom domain or investing unnecessarily in infrastructure that goes beyond what I need. Rather than overengineering a portfolio, I'd rather keep it practical, maintainable, and focused on presenting my work.

Ultimately, this is a collaborative process between human creativity and AI assistance, with GitHub Pages providing the platform and me maintaining control over the final product. The website is an ongoing project and a reflection of how I approach problem-solving: making deliberate choices, learning as I go, and using the tools available to achieve a practical result.
